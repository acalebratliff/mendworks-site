Most ideas are declined after a careful look, and that's the point of looking. Before we build anything, we check whether a free tool already does the job, whether someone is already working on it, and whether building it would be fair to the people who make it now. The reasons below are about fit: every project here is someone's honest work.

Each hunt starts with a broad screen, and most candidates fall at that first step. This page lists most of the candidates we declined after a closer look, usually after our red team, and the hunts we closed without a candidate. Candidates still held or watched, and work in progress, are not listed.

Where a public link exists, the entry links to it. Some evidence lives in our internal research notes; those entries say so.
